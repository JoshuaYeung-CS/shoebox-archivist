# Examples

All names, folders and dates below are made up for illustration.

## Requests that start this Skill

- "Organize my scanned family photos in the Shoebox Scans folder."
- "Date these old scans and make albums, but show me first."
- "When was this photo taken?" (with one image: single-print path, no writes)
- "Make albums from the shoebox scans and write a little story for each."
- "Undo the last shoebox run."

## Requests that do not belong here

- "Delete the blurry scans." Deleting is out of scope; say so.
- "Who is this person?" about an unnamed face. The Skill does not identify people.
- "Find duplicates across the whole NAS." Only the confirmed folder is used.

## A worked run

**User:** Organize the scans in Family Archive, Shoebox 2. Dad says they are mostly from the 70s.

**Skill:** Asks for the mode (plan or apply), whether to correct file dates, and where to save stories, in one message. The user picks apply, file dates on, default story folder.

**Steps 0 to 5:** The photo service is healthy. The inventory finds 48 images and no ledger. Two images are duplicate scans. Step 3 returns 46 records: 23 fronts and 23 backs. Pairing by file name matches 21 pairs; two backs follow their fronts in scan order. Two people groups the user named earlier, Rose and Walt, appear in 15 prints.

**Step 6 evidence for scan-0007:** the back reads "Lake Tahoe Aug 74" in pen; the lab stamp says AUG 1974; metadata says a flatbed scanner on the day of scanning, so it is ignored; the era estimate is the 1970s. Proposed date 1974-08, level Month.

**Step 7 plan (excerpt):**

- Summary: Shoebox 2, 48 images, 23 prints with backs paired. Day 4, Month 7, Year 8, Decade 3, Undated 1.
- Album 1: 1974-08 Lake Tahoe. Evidence: back caption "Lake Tahoe Aug 74"; lab stamp AUG 1974; era 1970s. People: Rose, Walt. Prints: scan-0007, scan-0009, scan-0011. File dates: 15 Aug 1974 12:00.
- Album 2: 1977 Christmas. Evidence: back caption "Xmas 77". Level Month (holiday fixes December).
- Question 1: scan-0031 imprint reads 5 6 '78. May 6 or June 5? Scan-0033 from the same roll reads 6 23 '78, so month first is likely. Confirm?
- Not dated: scan-0045 (no text, era only 1960s or 1970s).
- Will change: 6 albums, 22 prints added, 6 stories drafted, 19 file dates, 1 file tagged Shoebox date unknown, 1 ledger note. Nothing deleted, moved or renamed.

**User:** Approve all, answer 1 month first.

**Step 8:** The ledger is saved first. No album with these names exists. Six albums are created and their identifiers returned. Nineteen file dates are set. One file is tagged. Six story drafts are shown; the user edits one word; six notes are saved. The closing ledger is saved.

**Story for album 1 (sample):**

> 1974-08 Lake Tahoe
>
> In August 1974, Rose and Walt spent time at Lake Tahoe. One print is labelled in pen "Lake Tahoe Aug 74". The photos show the two of them on a wooden dock and by a campfire at dusk.
>
> Date: 1974-08 (Month, from the back of scan-0007 and its lab stamp)

**Final report:** 48 images, 23 prints dated (Day 5, Month 7, Year 7, Decade 3, Undated 1), 6 albums with identifiers and counts, 6 story files, 19 file dates changed, 1 file tagged, 2 duplicates listed, the ledger path, and "say undo the last shoebox run to reverse the file dates and tag".
